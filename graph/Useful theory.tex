\textbf{Erdös-Gallai theorem}: A simple graph with node degrees $d_1 \geq \ldots \geq d_n$ exists
iff $d_1+\ldots+d_n$ is even and for every $k=1,\ldots,n$
\[
    \sum_{i=1}^{k}d_i \leq k(k-1) + \sum_{i=k+1}^{n} \min(d_i,k)
\]
\textbf{Euler's formula}: if a finite, connected, planar graph is drawn in the plane without any edge 
intersections, and $v$ is the number of vertices, $e$ is the number of edges and $f$ is the number of 
faces (regions bounded by edges, including the outer, infinitely large region), then
\[
    v-e+f = 2
\]
